Abdul Khaliq

PhD Student, Water Science & Management

New Mexico State University

I am a PhD student in Water Science & Management at New Mexico State University. My research is in environmental, energy, and resource economics.

I use applied econometric methods to study renewable energy policy, land-use governance, energy markets, and environmental outcomes. My current doctoral training also extends this work to questions involving water and natural resources.
